CHRISTMAS SPECTACULAR

About the Show

Join us for this heartwarming holiday tale! The Christmas Spectacular brings seasonal cheer to the stage with festive dance choreography, theatrical acting, and beautiful musical performances from our studio performers.

Celebrate the holiday season with family and friends at Bristol Community College. Featuring classic holiday music, gorgeous costume displays, and inspiring performances, this is a community event you won't want to miss.

Ticketing lines and online reservations will be open closer to the performance dates. Check back here or sign up for our studio emails to be notified as soon as tickets go on sale.
