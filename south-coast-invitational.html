SOUTH COAST INVITATIONAL DANCE COMPETITION

About the Event

South Coast Invitational is more than just a dance competition — it is a celebration of artistry, passion, education, and community. Designed to provide a positive and inspiring experience for dancers, teachers, and studios, South Coast Invitational brings performers together in a supportive and professional environment where talent, creativity, and dedication can shine on stage.

Our mission is to create an event that celebrates the love of dance while encouraging young performers to grow both artistically and personally. From outstanding performances to meaningful educational opportunities, South Coast Invitational is committed to making every dancer feel valued and inspired.

Teachers and studio directors are invited to join us for an exciting and memorable competition experience at Bristol Community College.

More Than Just a Competition

South Coast Invitational proudly supports the future goals and educational aspirations of young performers by offering college scholarships to graduating high school seniors.

Each participating studio is eligible to nominate one graduating senior to receive a $1,000 college scholarship.

South Coast Invitational College Scholarship Program

South Coast Invitational is honored to support the next generation of performers as they continue their educational and artistic journeys.

Scholarship Eligibility Requirements

  1. Applicants must be graduating high school seniors from the Class of 2027.
  2. Scholarship funds must be applied toward attendance at an accredited college, university, or educational program.
  3. Scholarship payments will be issued directly to the college or educational institution selected by the recipient.
  4. Participating studios must have a minimum of:
    • 15 student competitors
    • 15 competition entries
    in order to qualify for scholarship participation.
  5. South Coast Invitational accepts a maximum of five studios per season in order to maintain a positive, organized, and personalized competition experience.

Scholarship Application Process

  1. Complete the Scholarship Application
    Graduating seniors must complete and submit the official South Coast Invitational Scholarship Application Form.
  2. Submission Deadline
    All completed scholarship applications must be submitted by: March 15, 2027.
  3. Performance Requirements
    Graduating seniors are not required to perform a solo to be considered for the scholarship. Judges will be notified of all competition routines in which scholarship applicants are participating.

We look forward to celebrating the achievements, dedication, and passion of our talented students.
