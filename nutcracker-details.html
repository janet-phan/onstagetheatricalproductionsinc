NUTCRACKER BALLET

About the Show

A holiday classic featuring our talented dancers. On Stage Theatrical Productions is proud to present the annual production of the Nutcracker Ballet. Join Clara on her magical journey through the Land of Sweets, filled with toy soldiers, dancing snowflakes, and the Sugar Plum Fairy.

Our dancers have been working tirelessly to bring this classical masterpiece to life. This production features students of all ages and showcase their progression in classical ballet technique, musicality, and performance artistry.

Detailed cast list, rehearsal schedules, and additional performance notes will be updated on this page as the production approaches.
